Student experience

Build skills for research, transfer, and STEM careers.

Students can connect psychology and neuroscience with technical, research, teamwork, and communication skills that transfer across academic and professional settings.

Technical skills

Research skills

Literature review, question development, study design, measurement, data collection, ethical research, analysis, and scientific reporting.

Professional skills

Teamwork, documentation, presentations, problem solving, scientific communication, and collaboration across disciplines.
