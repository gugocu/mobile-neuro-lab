The Central Arizona College Mobile Neuro Lab introduces students to EEG, brain–computer interfaces, research methods, and emerging neurotechnology through accessible, hands-on experiences. The Mobile Neuro Lab connects psychology and neuroscience with practical technology, research training, and STEM workforce development at Central Arizona College. 🧠 EEG & brain signals Build and work with EEG systems, learn how brain signals are recorded, and develop foundational skills in neural data collection. 💻 Neurotechnology Explore brain–computer interfaces, OpenBCI technology, data workflows, and the rapidly growing field of neurotechnology. 🔬 Research training Develop skills in hypotheses, study design, measurement, ethics, data collection, analysis, and scientific communication.
